# Changelog

## v2.0.0-rc1

- Add a printed preload collar and thrust plate to take up wrist-interface axial clearance after assembly.
- Add a side-accessible M3 pinch screw and captive ordinary hex nut; use M3×20 for all five new screws.
- Match the retaining ears to the wrist groove's rounded ends; increase ear thickness to 1.8 mm.
- Provide three thread-clearance options, matching fit coupons, STEP and print-oriented STL/3MF.
- Verify CAD/STL agreement, thread insertion, fastener/tool access, sampled shell placement, arm clearance and generic toolpaths.
- Add English and Chinese documentation, source attribution, reproducible checks and a physical acceptance worksheet.

Physical fit, preload retention, strength and ABS creep remain untested. This is a release candidate, not a qualified load-bearing product.

## v0.2

Archived single-piece fit prototype. Improved screw/washer pockets and documented assembly order, reference toolpaths and remaining theoretical free play. Available through the `v0.2` tag and release.
